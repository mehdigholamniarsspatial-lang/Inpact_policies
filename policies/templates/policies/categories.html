How each policy is scored

Policy intensity multiplies three scores. Two are fixed for each policy; one changes every year.

Implementation (changes by year)

If a matching OECD score exists, implementation is that score divided by 10. Otherwise it follows an assumed ramp-up from the start year. The speed of the ramp-up depends on the type of mechanism.

0–1OECD stringency ÷ 10, or assumed timing

Coverage score (fixed)

How much of the sector the policy reaches. It is an ordinal score (a rank on a fixed scale), not a measured share of emissions.
