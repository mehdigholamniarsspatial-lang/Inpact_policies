How each policy is scored

Policy intensity multiplies three components. Two are fixed judgement scores per policy; one changes by year.

Implementation (changes by year)

Where a matching OECD CAPMF score exists, implementation is that score divided by 10. Otherwise an assumed ramp-up from the start year is used, depending on the policy's mechanism class.

0–1OECD stringency ÷ 10, or assumed timing

Coverage score (fixed)

How much of the sector the policy reaches. An ordinal judgement, not a measured share of emissions.
