Policy instruments

Governments reduce greenhouse-gas emissions through a mix of instruments that work in different ways. Some change prices, some set rules, some fund investment and some build institutions. This guide explains each type of instrument in the dataset: its economic rationale, how Ireland has applied it, its known strengths and limitations, and how it is measured here.
