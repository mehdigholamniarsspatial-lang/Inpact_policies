OECD policy-stringency scores

The OECD Climate Actions and Policies Measurement Framework (CAPMF) rates how stringent each type of climate policy is in Ireland, from 0 (none) to 10 (most stringent), every year from 1990 to 2023. It covers {{ level3_count }} policy categories and {{ level4_count }} detailed indicators. This dataset uses the scores to measure how far matched policies were implemented each year.

Four levels

Level 1: sectoral, cross-sectoral and international. Level 2: sector by market-based or other instruments. Level 3: policy categories such as carbon taxes or building codes. Level 4: specific indicators, such as the carbon tax on buildings.

Observation flags

A normal value, E estimated (kept), K included in another category, M cannot exist. K and M values are treated as missing and never filled in from other years.

How the scores are used

A policy's implementation level is its matched score divided by 10 in the same year. Of 47 source categories with a positive score, 20 were eligible and anchor named policies; the rest were excluded, with the reason recorded below.
